### Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion diced
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 tablespoon curry powder
- 1.5 cup ketchup
- 2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 teaspoon paprika
- 3 tablespoon light brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
- Pinch of cayenne
- curry powder for sprinkling

### Steps

1. Pan on medium heat
2. Fry onions in the oil until translucent
3. Once translucent add garlic for 3 minutes until strongly fragrant
    - Try not to brown the onion but swapping the second the onion begins to brown is ideal
4. Add in curry powder and stir for 30 seconds
5. Add in the ketchup and all other ingredients
6. Blend
